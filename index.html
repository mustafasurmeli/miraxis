Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what information MIRAXIS (com.surmeli.miraxis) collects, why, and who it is shared with.

1. Information we collect

The app collects the following automatically while you use it:

  • Advertising ID. The identifier Android assigns to your device for advertising.
  • Gameplay data. Scores, session length, puzzle progress and in-game actions.
  • Crash and diagnostic data. Error reports with device model, Android version and app version.
  • Language and region. Your device language and general region, used to show the app in one of its 15 languages.
  • Purchase status. If you buy Remove Ads or Skin Lock, Google Play tells the app which product you bought and whether the purchase went through. We never receive your card number, billing address or other payment details.

The app has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location or photos.

2. How we use it

  • To improve the game and fix bugs. Firebase Analytics shows how the game is played; Firebase Crashlytics reports crashes.
  • To show ads. Google AdMob shows banner, interstitial, rewarded and native ads, and may use your Advertising ID to choose them.

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Your choice about personalized ads

The first time you open MIRAXIS, you are asked whether ads may be personalized. If you decline, ads are still shown, but they are not based on your Advertising ID. You can also turn off ad personalization or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device).

4. Third-party services

Each of these services has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing (purchases) policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data stored on your device

Your progress, saved games, scores, achievements, settings and daily streak are kept on your device. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include them in your Google account backup. Notifications are scheduled on the device itself; no data is sent to send them.

6. Data transfer

Information collected by the services above may be processed on Google servers in the United States and other countries. Google states that it complies with applicable data protection frameworks, including the GDPR.

7. Data retention

  • Analytics data: 14 months.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to delete data associated with your device, or ask any question about this policy, by writing to the address below.

9. Children

MIRAXIS does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that such data was collected, we will delete it as soon as possible.
